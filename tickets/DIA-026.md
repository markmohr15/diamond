# DIA-026 — The putout belongs to whoever had the ball at the base (§15.1)
**Spec:** §15.1 (the throw, the force prompt), §4.3 (`putoutTouchId`). Spec v0.66.
**Sequencing note:** **M1**, under the same gate as DIA-022: a play that enters cleanly and
derives wrongly is an M1 blocker under "99.9% of possible plays score correctly".
**Status:** built on `dia-026-putout-by-base`.

## Why

Mark, 2026-11-18, from the simulator: a grounder to the 3B with a runner on first. He marked the
runner out at second, then dragged the 2B to the bag. The out took its putout from whoever held
the ball when it was marked, so the play read *3B unassisted*, and the 2B's catch was recorded after
the out she made. Entering the throw first worked: the force prompt asks at the bag.

Mark: *"we have this dynamic where the user can enter where the ball is and where the runners go
but the timing of them may or may not be in order."*

## What was ruled

- **Location, not tap order.** A force or an appeal is made by whoever has the ball at the base.
  An out whose putout touch was made more than 15 feet from the base (the force prompt's radius)
  is open.
- **The next throw caught at that base claims it.** She takes the putout, the out moves to just
  after her catch, and the thrower keeps the assist. Out-first and throw-first record the same play.
- **Unclaimed stays put.** The SS who fields it and runs to the bag herself made it alone.
- **Tags are not reopened.** The `tag_applied` touch names who tagged.

## Built

- `PlayDraft.claimingOpenPutout`, called by `receiveThrow` after the reception.
- `baseCoordinate`, `baseCoverRadiusFt` and `coversBase` in `field_profile.dart`. `FieldGeometry`
  and the force prompt now read these, so the two orders agree on who was covering.
- **Tests:**
  - out-first reads the same as throw-first;
  - the SS who takes it to the bag herself keeps the putout;
  - a putout already made at the bag isn't claimed by the double-play relay;
  - a tag is never claimed.
- **No new fixture.** The stream an out-first entry now writes is the throw-first stream, which
  fixtures already cover.

## Known residual

A tag entered before the throw still credits the holder's tag. If that turns up, move the minted
`tag_applied` to the receiver the same way.
