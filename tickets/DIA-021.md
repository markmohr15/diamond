# DIA-021 — The pitch-entry rail, and one labeled undo (§10.3, §11.1, §11.2, §15.2)
**Spec:** §10.3 (the call), §11.1 (the loop), §11.2 (the mode ladder), §15.2, §15.5, §19.4
**Design:** `Pitch Screen.dc.html` in Claude Design, turn 10 ("Mode gone, one number"), artboards
10A/10B. The token expires — see the note at the end.
**Sequencing note:** **M1.** Mark, 2026-09-02: *"I'm trying to make M1 to the level that a user could
use it in a completely functional way."* This is the entry surface itself, so it is upstream of
DIA-009's manual script — the script should run against the rail, not against what the rail replaces.
**Goal:** replace the loop's implicit call → location → outcome sequence with the design's explicit
three-step rail, give the top-bar undo a label naming what it will undo, and move per-pitch cancel
onto each screen.

## Why

Two problems arrived from opposite directions and turn out to be one.

**From the simulator:** *"If we click Skip Call, there needs to be a way to go back and make a call."*
Verified — mid-plate-appearance, tapping undo after "Skip call" **voids the pitch you already
recorded** (the count went 1-0 back to 0-0) and leaves you on the location canvas. On the first pitch
of a plate appearance it is merely disabled, and the canvas's `Cancel` is the only way back.

**From the design:** the top bar's control is now a labeled pill —

```
↺  UNDO PITCH 3
```

— and there is **no `✕` in the top bar at all**; it disappears between turn 9 and turn 10, exactly as
the label appears. Cancel moves into each screen's bottom bar as `Discard pitch`, beside `Back to
game`.

Mark, relaying the design: *"the buttons to move around pitch call and pitch location need to be
separate but need to be connected to the other buttons related to this, and then undo at the top
should purely be related to plays (or pitches) in the game and be unrelated to calls."*

That resolves the first problem by removing its premise. **There is no Skip call to go back from.**
Step 3 is live from the start — solid border, *"Jump here — no call"* — and tapping it drops steps 1
and 2 and opens the zone for a pitch nobody called. The design's own prose says that "replaced the
floating skip button."

## What the design specifies (10A/10B)

- **The rail**, left side: `1 CALL PITCH` · `2 RELAY CODE` · `3 RECORD LOCATION`, with the current
  step marked (`STEP 1 OF 3`) and step 3 always live.
- **The call screen is a menu of spots**, not codes: six callable cells as plain Grass fills on a
  hatched zone, no numbers on the field. Tap one and 10B shows the single number — `702` at 78px on
  Ink, labelled `YELL THIS` — with the pitch and spot small underneath.
- **The code card is the commit button.** Tapping `702` confirms you relayed it, which folds the old
  step 2 into this screen. The design notes the rail still lists it "so you can see the shape of the
  entry, but it may not need to be a stop."
- **MODE is off the screen.** The rail says where you are, and the one thing mode did — log a pitch
  without calling it — is step 3 being live.
- **Bottom bar:** `Back to game`, `Discard pitch`, the status (`PITCH 4 · CALLING`, `DROP · NO SPOT
  PICKED`), and the primary action (`Relayed it →`).

## Slices

**021a — the labeled undo.** Smallest, and independently useful. `↺ UNDO PITCH 3` instead of a bare
icon. It dissolves an ambiguity DIA-019 kept running into: a bare `↺` could mean the play, the pitch,
or the entry step, and when it greyed out it explained nothing. A button that names its target cannot
be misread, and a *bounded* undo (§11.3 v0.54's seal) is a different experience when you can see what
it is offering.

**021b — cancel moves per-screen.** `Discard pitch` in each pitch screen's bottom bar; the top-bar
`✕` goes.

> **Constraint: 021b and the field's own controls move together.** DIA-019c removed the field
> surface's `↺` and `✕` and made the top-bar pair serve every surface. If the `✕` leaves the top bar
> before the field has its own control back, there is a window with **no way to restart a play**.
> Whether the field's step-back also returns to the field is DIA-018's call — the design file covers
> the pitch screens only and says nothing about the field.

**021c — the rail.** The three steps, step 3 live from the start, mode gone.

**021d — the call screen as spots.** Green cells, then the one number. Larger than the rest and the
part most likely to want its own design pass.

## What this partly reverses

DIA-019c unified undo and cancel into the top bar, on the reading that one control everywhere beats
two on two layers. The design goes the other way: **one labeled game-level undo above, per-surface
controls below.** That is a different shape, and 019c was still worth doing — it fixed a real defect
where the top-bar button reached past the surface and voided the pitch under an open play, and it
built the step-undo the field needs either way. But it is a way station, and this ticket is where it
lands.

## Open

- **Whether step 2 is a stop.** The design says the code card being the commit button may make it
  redundant.
- **The theme toggle** sits left of undo (DIA-019g) and appears nowhere in 10A. That placement was
  Mark's instruction, not the design's; it needs a home when the top bar is rebuilt.

## Accept

The rail is on screen and step 3 is live from the start; there is no Skip call button; the top-bar
undo names its target; `Discard pitch` is per-screen and the top-bar `✕` is gone **with the field's
own control restored in the same change**; DIA-009's manual script runs against this surface.
