# DIA-008 — Field canvas + play chain strip (M1 subset)
**Spec:** §15, §16.3 (static profile), §4.2–4.3, §15.6 (v0.42)
**Slices:** built as four PRs — **a** field canvas core: landing + trajectory + runner-advance
drags + atomic commit + crash journal (`dia-008a-field-canvas-core`); **b** chain strip + fielder
touches/throws/outs + modifier chips + prompted misplay (fixtures 01–03); **c** D3K resolution +
fast paths (play #5); **d** the idle field + between-pitch runner events (§15.6 v0.42).
**Goal:** field canvas rendered from a hardcoded FieldProfile (fastpitch 12U preset); landing tap w/ live
distance; trajectory row; fielder tap = touch, drag = throw; runner drag to base/out; chain strip with
"+" modifier chips (misplay set + rule-call subset: obstruction, interference_runner); atomic commit (✓),
uncommitted-state journal + restore. (The §15.4.2 "safe at first but ball beat her" prompt was
built and then **withdrawn in v0.43**: without a timing model it cannot tell a dropped throw from
a bang-bang infield single, so it guessed on every close play — and it fired against the
batter-runner's presumed walk-up rather than any ruling. The drop stays one tap on the node that
took the throw.)
**Added (§11.3 v0.41):** the D3K resolution flow, moved here from DIA-007 because its arming keys on
the catch, not the pitch — uncaught = `swinging_strike_blocked` OR any third strike (called or
swinging) carrying a catcher-misplay touch, and the misplay entry surface is this ticket's play
chain. Four one-tap fast paths for plays ending at first (Out tag → `RunnerOut{1, tag}`; Out throw →
`RunnerOut{1, strikeout_d3k_throw}`; Safe wild pitch → `RunnerAdvance{0→1, dropped_third_strike}`
alone; Safe passed ball → `FielderTouch{2, missed_catch, OE:true}` anchored to the pitch + the same
advance, `←touch`), full chain for the rest. The loop currently records the strike-three out
unconditionally; this flow must convert or replace that cleanly.
**Added (§15.6 v0.42) — slice d:** the idle field. A field affordance on the loop's idle surfaces
opens the canvas with no ball in play: defense + runners visible. Runner drag + reason chip
commits a between-pitch event immediately (no ✓, no journal) — advances `stolen_base` (default) /
`wild_pitch` / `passed_ball` / `defensive_indifference`; outs `caught_stealing` (default) /
`picked_off`. PB emits the §13.2 pair anchored to the most recent pitch (later runners in the same
window reuse the touch); WP is the advance alone. Each entry is its own action-scoped undo unit —
requires the undo-root mechanism to stop keying on event type alone (a `RunnerAdvance` is a
consequence after a walk, a root when scorer-authored here).
**Accept:** each of fixtures 01–03 can be ENTERED through the UI in ≤ the tap counts documented in the
ticket's test script, producing streams that pass DIA-004's assertions. Play #5 (D3K throw-away)
enterable end-to-end. Kill-app-mid-play restores canvas. Slice d: a steal enterable in 3 gestures
(open field · drag · chip), CS likewise; one undo reverses exactly that entry, never the pitch
before it.
**Out of scope:** alignment drag (§16.4, M2 — the idle field renders standard spots until then),
presets, foul-ball field taps.

## Test script — UI entry sequences and tap budgets (draft)

Conventions: a *gesture* is one tap or one drag. Every play opens with §11.1's pitch entry —
type, zone, ✓, actual location, outcome — **5 taps** to `in_play` in full mode (the budget the
accept criteria measure; skip-location paths are cheaper). Field-entry gestures are counted
separately below; the ✓ commit counts as one. Budgets are ceilings the integration tests
assert, not targets. Draft status: expected to be revised as the surfaces land (a/b/c slices).

Field entry follows §15.1 v0.43: the trajectory modal opens with the surface; ball path drawn
(two taps, accent streak) or implied by the fielder drag (live, she rides the finger) + the
what-happened popup; the batter opens already walked up to first (forced cascade); runners
throws are one tap — the ball held (accent ring), tap the receiver — with the route drawn as
dashed segments; runners resolve on the SAFE/OUT pair at the approached base, each release
classified by a popup with
disjoint vocabularies (safe: hit itself / on the throw / on an error / fielder's choice /
obstruction; out: force/tag/fly-out + interference). All popups are centered modals.

**Play 01 — dropped liner, out anyway (8 field gestures; 13 total)**

1. trajectory modal: `Line drive`
2. drag 6 to where she played it (ball location implied; she repositions)
3. popup: `Dropped` (touch + `location`; OE defaults true, §13.2; claims the batter's
   provisional reach → `error`, `←touch`)
4. tap 6 (the pickup, where she stands)
5. popup: `Picked it up` (`fielded`)
6. tap 1B — the ball is held, so a tap throws it (`received_throw` at her spot); the
   throw arrived at first with the batter heading there, so the SAFE/OUT pair pops at
   the bag
7. tap **OUT** (the force play, resolved; absorbs her walk-up leg; putout ← the throw)
8. ✓

**Play 02 — boot, then throw sails; batter to third (10 field gestures; 15 total)**
`Grounder` · drag SS to the ball · popup `Booted` (claims the reach → advance `error`,
`←touch`) · tap SS · popup `Fielded` (the recovery) · tap the new SS node · `Wild throw` chip
(retypes) · drag batter-runner 1B → **SAFE** at 3B · popup `On an error` (links the wild
throw → advance `wild_throw`, `←touch`) · ✓

**Play 03 — single, R3 scores, batter out stretching 9-6 (9 field gestures; 14 total)**
`Line drive` · drag RF to the ball · popup `Picked it up` (clean — the batter's walk-up stands
as `batted_ball`) · drag r3 → **SAFE** at home · popup `On the hit` · drag ball RF → SS
(`received_throw`) · drag batter-runner 1B → **OUT** at 2B · popup `Tag` (inferred, leading —
no force live — emitting `tag_applied` + `RunnerOut`) · ✓

**The simple 6-3 (5 gestures):** `Grounder` · drag SS to the ball · popup `Fielded` · tap 1B
(the throw; SAFE/OUT pops at the bag) · tap **OUT** · ✓

**Home run (4 gestures):** `Fly ball` · tap the landing beyond the fence · `Home run?` **Yes**
(everyone aboard scores; RBIs derived) · ✓.

**Ground-rule double (5 gestures):** `Line drive` · tap the landing in the park · tap the end
point beyond the fence · `Ground rule double?` **Yes** (two bases apiece) · ✓. **No** on either
question proceeds as an ordinary play (§16.3).

**Doubled off (7 gestures):** `Fly ball` · drag CF to the ball · popup `Caught` (runners return
to their bases) · tap SS (the throw to second) · drag the runner → **OUT** at her base ·
`Didn't tag up` · ✓.

**Routine fly out (4 gestures):** `Fly ball` · drag CF to the ball · popup `Caught` (voids the
walk-up, records the fly out automatically, putout ← catch) · ✓

**Catcher's interference (1 tap, on the pitch loop):** the outcome dialog's
`Catcher's interference` — dead ball, PA over, batter awarded first with the forced chain, ⚖
emitted, E2 derived (§4.1 v0.43). Never reaches the field surface.

**Play 05 — D3K throw-away (7 resolution gestures; ~14 total)**
Pitch entry ends `swinging_strike_blocked` (dirt-band hinge adds up to 2 taps: bounce depth
or "depth unknown"; override row 1 tap). Loop records the K and the out; arming (2 outs,
§11.3) opens the resolution surface. Not a fast-path play, so full chain: drag batter-runner
→ 1B (advance `dropped_third_strike`; converts the recorded out) · tap 2 · +chip ·
`wild_throw` · drag batter-runner → 2B (`←touch`) · drag r1 → 3B (`←touch`) · ✓

**D3K fast paths (1 tap each):** Out (tag) · Out (throw) · Safe (wild pitch) · Safe (passed
ball) — the tap is the commit; no ✓.

**Journal restore:** enter play 03 through gesture 6, kill the app, relaunch → field canvas
restores with landing, trajectory, and the partial chain intact; finish and commit; stream
passes the fixture's assertions.

**Between-pitch events (slice d, 4 gestures each — the ✓ commits, v0.45):**
steal — field affordance · drag r1 → 2B · chip `SB`;
caught stealing — field affordance · drag r1 → out at 2B · chip `CS`;
passed ball, R3 scores — field affordance · drag r3 → home · chip `PB` · ✓ (emits the §13.2 pair).
One undo after any of these reverses exactly that entry.
