# DIA-029 — Runners off the bag, a throw asks about the runner behind it, and the tag (§15.1)
**Spec:** §15.1 (the walk-up, the force-play pills, the tag), §15.6 (between pitches). Spec v0.73,
v0.74.
**Sequencing note:** **M1.** The fourth clause: DIA-009's script should run without Mark needing a
workaround, and dragging a runner just so a throw can ask about her is one.
**Status:** built on `dia-029-runners-off-the-bag`.

## Why

Mark, 2026-11-19: the SAFE/OUT pair came up on a throw only for a runner the walk-up had presumed
into motion. *"Say a grounder to SS with a runner at second and we have the SS throw it to 3B. We
want Safe/Out to be options here without having to move the runner to second."* And a runner
standing on her bag drew on top of it: *"This clears up any confusion as far as base or runner if
they are separate."*

## What was ruled

- **A throw caught at a base asks about the runner on the base before it** when nobody is presumed
  headed there. Nothing moved her, so each pill is the drag to that base and asks what the drag
  asks. That holds between pitches too, where the ruling is asked (v0.69).
- **On a batted ball, a runner nothing forced stands off her bag**, a sixth of the way toward the
  next base. A forced runner still runs a third of the way. Mark chose the shorter lead so *off the
  bag* doesn't read as *running*. *"With no force, we leave them where they are if the user doesn't
  move them."*
- **The tag (v0.74).** The fielder with the ball dragged over a runner off her bag, or that runner
  dragged onto her, turns whichever is under the finger into the OUT pill. Releasing records the
  out, with the putout hers. Holding 2 s hides it silently, and the release just puts the fielder
  there. Only runners off their bags count: *"runners should only be on their bags now if a user
  advances them."*

## Built

- **`field_geometry.dart`:** `leadFraction` (1/6), and `runnerTokenCenter(leading:)`.
- **`RunnerToken.leading`:** set on a batted ball for a runner with no leg (`PlayDraft.hasLeg`). The
  painter and the hit test both read it.
- **`_forcePlayFor`:**
  - after the walk-up legs, it takes a runner standing on N−1;
  - the pending play records `standing`, and both of its pills route through `_resolveDrop`.
- **The tag:**
  - `_tagTarget` in the surface; the painter draws `tagTarget` in place of the token it covers;
  - the hold now holds a base pill or a tag target;
  - over a runner, the base pills stand down, even once her target hid.
- **Tests:**
  - the tag both ways records the same out, putout to the SS;
  - held 2 s, no out either way;
  - leaving and coming back restores the target;
  - a runner on her bag is no target;
  - golden scenarios for both directions;
  - Mark's play: short to third, with OUT as a tag and no question, and SAFE recording the leg and
    asking why;
  - a runner left alone commits no advance;
  - between pitches, the throw asks the ruling;
  - the geometry of the lead;
  - `dragToken` finds a runner standing off her bag.
