# DIA-028 — Out stretching, the putout chain, and a lone OUT answer (§13, §15.6)
**Spec:** §13 (hit rank, assists), §15.6 (the SAFE and OUT pills). Spec v0.71.
**Sequencing note:** **M1.** A play that enters cleanly but derives wrongly is an M1 blocker
(CLAUDE.md, the 99.9% clause). This one scored a double as a single.
**Status:** built on `dia-028-out-stretching`.

## Why

Mark, 2026-11-19, from the simulator: with a runner on second, a fly fell in in right-center, the
runner scored, and the batter was thrown out at third, CF to 2B to 3B. He entered the batter safe at
first and then out at third. That turned up four problems:

- The OUT dialog asked how she was out, with Tag as its only answer.
- The hit derived as a single, since nothing recorded her at second.
- The play line read "2B to 3B", naming only the last throw.
- In the variant where she took second on the throw, the line never said so: *"it's confusing how
  she's out at third on a single."*

## What was ruled

- **A lone OUT answer is not asked**, the same as SAFE. Between pitches there are always several
  answers, so that dialog still opens.
- **Out stretching.** A batter tagged out going into a base was safe at the one before it, and that
  base counts toward the hit. The exception is a leg of hers that already reaches it: that leg says
  how she got there and caps the hit (v0.43). Tags only.
- **The putout chain** is the fielders who handled the ball on the way to the putout, in order, and
  the play line and the assists both read it. A misplay starts it over.
  - The box credits an assist to everyone in the chain but the putout's fielder.
  - The line names two throws at most, the last three fielders: *"that would just be too long to
    read every throw."*
- **A batter's leg past her hit is said** right after the hit, with *on the throw* when a clean
  throw enabled it.

## Built

- **The OUT dialog** (`field_entry_surface.dart`) applies a lone answer straight away.
- **`official_scoring.dart`:**
  - the out-stretching rule in the hit-rank derivation;
  - `putoutChain`, shared by the assists and the play line.
- **`play_line.dart`:**
  - the chain, capped at three fielders;
  - the clause for a leg past the hit.
- **Discovered:** play-20's shortstop had been credited an assist for the throw he threw away.
  Restarting the chain at a misplay removes it. Mark approved this mid-build.
- **Tests:**
  - fixture play-28, this play: a double, RBI, CF to 2B to 3B;
  - fixture play-29: a single, second on the throw, out at third;
  - the fixture runner learns `assists`, and play-20 asserts them;
  - play-line tests for the two-throw cap, the misplay restart, and a single plus the throw;
  - a widget test for the batter out at third with no dialog;
  - four field tests updated so they no longer tap a lone Tag chip.
