# DIA-013 — Replace the batter silhouette with a realistic figure
**Spec:** §11.4, §18.7
**Sequencing note:** not in M1 and gates nothing. DIA-011 ships a working silhouette; this only
improves how it looks.

**Goal:** replace the batter silhouette's *shape* with something that reads convincingly as a batter
in a stance. DIA-011 draws it procedurally from round-capped strokes — one uniform-width tube per
limb — which cannot produce a body: nothing tapers, and where limbs meet they blend into a single
mass rather than reading as a shoulder or a hip. The geometry around it is correct and is not what
this ticket changes.

**What must survive unchanged.** The figure is *derived from `ZoneProfile`*, and that property is
the whole reason it is allowed on the entry canvas at all (§11.4 v0.36): the knee sits at `y = 0`
and the armpit at `y = 1` — definitionally the zone's own edges — so the batter tracks the zone rect
for every profile and cannot contradict it. Whatever replaces the drawing anchors to those same two
landmarks. `BatterSilhouette` and its tests should mostly survive; the painter's body is what goes.

Also unchanged: frontal plane only, no pointer events, handedness as a single mirror, and gender
neutral.

**Approaches worth exploring** — deliberately open, and worth trying more than one before choosing:
- **Traced alpha PNG.** Cut a real batting stance out of a photo, ship it as an asset, tint it at
  draw time. Cheapest to get right and needs no new dependency; adds `assets/`, a `pubspec.yaml`
  entry, and asset availability in golden tests. Needs the knee and armpit rows measured out of the
  image once, as constants, to anchor it.
- **Hand-authored outline path.** One closed contour in normalized units (fractions of stature) with
  real anatomy in it — back tapering to waist, thigh into calf, the notch under the arm, a head
  distinct from the shoulders. No asset, recolours freely, scales cleanly; perhaps 25–30 control
  points to author and tune.
- **Filled tapered polygons** rather than strokes — a middle option, better than the current tubes
  and much less work than a real outline, but unlikely to clear §18.7's "serviceable is the failure
  bar".

**Source note:** the reference used during DIA-011 was a screenshot of a commercial game. A generic
batting-stance silhouette is not really protectable, but tracing a specific commercial render is
closer to derivative work than a shipping product should rely on without deciding so consciously. A
stock softball-batter photo, or one Mark takes, sidesteps it and fits the sport being built for.

**Accept:** `showBatterSilhouette` defaults to on once the figure is good; the figure reads as a batter in a stance at tablet size in daylight (Mark's judgement, on
device — no golden can settle it); knee still lands on the zone's bottom edge and armpit on its top,
across at least two zone profiles; still takes no pointer events; still mirrors correctly for both
`batterSide` values; goldens updated.

**Turning the batter off is likely to become a real feature, not just a gate.** A future "practice"
state — pitchers throwing bullpens with no batter in the box — would want the silhouette hidden, and
that is a user-facing choice rather than a build-time one. So whatever visibility switch exists
should be shaped as something a setting can drive, not a constant to delete once the art is good.

**Out of scope:** the geometry — position off the chalk, feet equidistant from the box line and
separated in depth, ground projection of the feet, frame-cut head. All of that is DIA-011's and
correct. Also out of scope: animating the figure, per-batter *body* proportions beyond the stature
that `ZoneProfile` already implies, and showing it in the top-down plane.
