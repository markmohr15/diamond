# DIA-027 — Undo takes the whole entry, and Back one only goes backward (§6, §15.5)
**Spec:** §6 (undo), §11.3 (action roots), §15.5 (Back one), §15.6 (between-pitch entries). Spec
v0.70.
**Sequencing note:** **M1.** The count, outs and bases are never wrong (§11.2). An undo that
leaves half an entry standing, then saves it twice on recommit, breaks that.
**Status:** built on `dia-027-undo-whole-entry`.

## Why

Mark, 2026-11-18, from the simulator: a steal of second and a wild pitch to third, entered with one
✓, then undo. Only the wild pitch was voided, and the steal stayed. The reopened copy held both, so
committing it again would have saved the steal twice. The undo walk still treated each
between-pitch advance as its own action, from before v0.45 made a between-pitch entry a chain, and
nothing in the record said which events one ✓ had saved.

Mark: *"we need to be able to discard it all. And then if we hit undo, we still need the option to
get rid of all of it at once. And if we get rid of the most recent part of what was entered, we
then need the option to remove the second most recent part."* He also found Back one offering a
redo: after removing a part from the chain strip, Back one's next step put it back.

## What was ruled

- **One action, one batch.** Every atomic append stamps a shared envelope `batchId`, and undo voids
  the whole batch. Mark chose this (option A) over grouping by a shared timestamp, or by the kept
  copy beside the stream.
- **Back one only goes backward.** Removing a part never becomes something Back one restores.
- **Discarding all** of a reopened entry is Start play over, then Leave field. Nothing new was
  built for it; it needed the reopened steps (DIA-018b-1, #76).

## Built

- **Schema:**
  - `batchId` on the envelope (`schema/envelope.schema.json`);
  - Dart and TS regenerated;
  - a nullable `batch_id` column in Drift, migration v3 → v4.
- **`appendAllPending`** stamps the batch with its first event's id and returns it.
- **`_undoUnit`:**
  - a batch holding an action root is the whole unit, and nothing before it goes;
  - a batch of consequences only (a D3K ending) keeps walking back to its pitch, as before;
  - events without a batch (single appends, and rows from before v0.70) walk as they did.
- **The kept copy** records its batch, and undo's reopen matches on it. Copies from before v0.70
  fall back to matching by anchor.
- **`removeEntry`** applies the removal to every saved step and drops any step left unchanged. The
  key counter is ignored in that comparison.
- **Tests:**
  - steal plus wild pitch undoes whole and reopens whole;
  - two runners on one passed ball undo whole;
  - two entries off one pitch undo one at a time, each reopening as itself;
  - undo, then Start play over, then Leave field saves nothing;
  - remove the last part, and Back one takes the part before it;
  - the DIA-018b-1 reopen test now expects exactly one steal;
  - the v3 → v4 migration keeps old rows with no batch.
