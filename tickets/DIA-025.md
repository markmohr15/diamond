# DIA-025 — Misses are misplays: the fielder's popup by whether the ball landed (§15.1, §13.2)
**Spec:** §15.1 (the what-happened popup), §4.2 (`booted`, `missed_catch`), §13.2 (error kinds),
§14 (Play #4's kind). Spec v0.64.
**Sequencing note:** **M1**, under the same gate as DIA-022: a play that enters cleanly and
derives wrongly is an M1 blocker under "99.9% of possible plays score correctly", and an error the
scorer can see but can't record is the same failure.
**Status:** built on `dia-025-misplay-menus`. The branch is cut from DIA-018b-1 (#71) because it
builds on spec v0.63 and the field surface.

## Why

Mark, 2026-11-11, from the simulator: a popup fell in short right-center, the RF got to it and
missed it, the CF picked it up and threw to second, and the batter was safe there. He wanted
**single and E9**. *Missed it* recorded no touch, so there was nothing to link the extra base to.

## What was ruled

- **In the air:** *Caught · Missed catch · Misplayed · Fielded*, plus *Deflected* on an infield
  liner. What separates the two misplays is whether the ball had landed. Glove contact doesn't
  matter: *"if you miss a flyball or popup you should catch, rather it hit your glove or not is
  immaterial."* After it lands, an air ball offers *Fielded · Misplayed*.
- **On the ground:** *Fielded · Bobbled · Booted · Missed · Deflected*. *"Missing a ball is
  different than bobbling it and both are candidates for errors, but not necessarily so for
  either."*
- **Labels drop "it",** including the between-pitch *Picked up* and *Missed*.
- **A missed ball off the bat is a fielding error.** Catching is for a throw or a pitch. What she
  was receiving is **derived** from the chain rather than split into new touch types. Mark asked
  whether the two should be told apart, and was content to derive it *"if you're confident we can
  get what we need."*

## Built

| Choice | Records |
| --- | --- |
| Missed catch | `dropped` |
| Misplayed | `booted` |
| Missed (ground) | `missed_catch` |
| Bobbled | `bobbled`, now offered on batted balls |

There are no new schema values.

- **`rules/received.dart`:** `receivedBy` returns pitch, thrown ball, or live ball. A throw means
  the fielder before her had the ball (fielded, caught, received a throw, bobbled, or a tag). A
  pickoff has no recorded thrower, so between pitches anyone but the catcher who plays the ball
  first is taking a throw.
- **Who reads it:**
  - `officialErrorKindOf` (the engine)
  - `PlayDraft.errorKindOf` (the SAFE chips)
  - `throwerInto`. It used to take *any* earlier fielder, so a CF on a ball the RF let past her read
    as taking a throw.
- **Labels:** `ballPlayLabel` is shared by the popup, the chain strip's retype menu, and Back one.
- **Fixtures:**
  - Play #4 changes from catching to fielding.
  - New play 26 (this popup: single + E9).
  - New play 27 (a grounder through the legs: reached on E6).

## Follow-up on the same PR (2026-11-12)

When Mark re-entered the play, two problems came up:
- Settled on first and sent to second, the app asked "How much did she earn?" with a single option.
- Dragged straight to second, the app hid *Single and an error* one dialog deeper.

**Ruled:** with one misplay on the play, the SAFE dialog shows the splits itself. A split with only
one possible answer is applied without asking. With two or more misplays, the scorer still names
the error first.

**Also ruled 2026-11-13:** *Misplayed* leaves the ball loose, not at the RF's feet. Before this,
the CF coming over recorded as a throw from the RF. A grounder's boot and a missed catch are
unchanged.

## Known residual

Tapping a fielder to say she doesn't have a ball she cleanly fielded is drawing-only and never
reaches the stream. If the next fielder then misses it, that miss reads as a throw. Rare. If it ever
matters, record the tap; don't change the touch types.
