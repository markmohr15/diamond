# DIA-031 — RBI credit for forced runs and fielder's choice (§13)
**Spec:** §13 (official scoring), §13.5 (why this is not an override case)
**Sequencing note:** **M1.** Mark's bar is that 99.9% of plays score correctly, and a bases-loaded
walk is common. Found during DIA-010 (2026-11-24); split out by Mark so DIA-010 stays about overrides.

## The bug

`official_scoring.dart`'s `_rbiReasons` credits a run only when its reason is `batted_ball` or
`ground_rule`. By rule the batter is also credited when a run scores on:
- a **bases-loaded walk**,
- a **bases-loaded HBP**,
- a **fielder's choice**, or on an out (a groundout that scores the runner from third).

Today every one of those derives **0 RBI**.

**A sacrifice fly on an error (Mark, 2026-11-24): the batter gets the RBI automatically.** A run
that a charged error enabled derives no RBI today, sacrifice or not. When the plate appearance is
ruled a sacrifice fly, the run scoring on it credits the batter even with the error. In practice
that means the scorer's explicit sacrifice flag and the clause-(2) sac fly, since §13.6's derived
sac fly already excludes any play with an error. A scorer could fix each with DIA-010's `RbiOverride`,
but §13.5 says that is the wrong fix: *a projection output that needs routine hand-correction is a
sign the derivation itself is wrong.*

## What stays excluded

No RBI when the run scores on:
- a charged error (already excluded);
- a **double play** — check how a ground-ball double play is represented before coding the
  exclusion;
- a wild pitch, passed ball, stolen base or defensive indifference.

## Open

- **Catcher's interference and obstruction with the bases loaded.** Whether either earns an RBI
  under the rule set we hardcode (fastpitch 12U, DIA-017) is to be looked up, not guessed.

## Accept

- Unit tests:
  - a bases-loaded walk is 1 RBI;
  - a bases-loaded HBP is 1 RBI;
  - a fielder's choice that scores the runner from third is 1 RBI;
  - a groundout that scores her is 1 RBI;
  - a double play that scores her is 0;
  - an error that scores her is 0;
  - a sacrifice fly with an error, where she scores on the error, is 1.
- No §14 fixture's expected output changes, unless the fixture is wrong, which is then flagged
  separately.
