# DIA-014 — The outcome sheet, rebuilt (§11.1)
**Spec:** §11.1, §11.2 (mode ladder), §4.1 (`batterAction` — schema change), §13.2, §15.6
**Sequencing note:** M1. Independent of DIA-009's acceptance script, but the script runs *through*
this sheet, so landing it first avoids rewriting the script's tap counts twice.
**Goal:** rebuild §11.1's ball/strike/in-play sheet on Claude Design panel **5A** ("Pitch result —
tiered"), delete the location suggestion, and make the modifier tier real.

## Why

The sheet ranks by **Diamond's location suggestion**, and that suggestion cannot do the job.
`suggestOutcome` only ever returns `BALL` or `CALLED_STRIKE` — location knows nothing about whether
the batter swung — and its most confident case is the worst one, since a pitch *in* the zone is more
often swung at than taken. It has been promoting the wrong button and calling it help.

5A ranks by **how often a thing gets tapped** instead, which is fixed, knowable, and honest.

## Decisions already made (Mark, 2026-08-23) — do not re-litigate

- **Five fixed primaries, equal prominence:** Ball · Called strike · Swinging · Foul · **In play**.
  Frequency order, not location order.
- **Delete the suggestion entirely** — `suggestOutcome`, `outcomeConfirmKey`, and the confirm button.
  Location is still captured and still feeds heat maps, the intent/actual delta, and analytics; it
  simply stops voting on the outcome.
- **In play follows 5A**: fifth primary, full width, **above** the rare chips. This supersedes an
  earlier instruction to put it in the modal's final row.
- **Rare outcomes stay** as a wrapped chip row under an "everything else" rule: HBP, catcher's
  interference, illegal pitch, foul tip, foul bunt, unknown.
- **No count-consequence subtitles** ("→ STRIKEOUT" vs "→ 2–2"). 5A's best idea, and not M1.
- **No design work of any kind** — no colors, radii, borders, shadows, or custom sizing. **Ordering
  and position only.** Prominence comes from the widget vocabulary already in the file
  (`FilledButton` for the primaries, `OutlinedButton` for the rest). The post-M1 design ticket
  restyles this structure without re-deciding it.

## The modifier tier (5A's "AND THEN")

This is the half that does not exist yet, and it is where the ticket earns its keep.

**`batterAction` — a schema change, not just UI.** §4.1 already defines the field ("observed
offensive posture on this pitch, orthogonal to outcome") with five values, and **nothing writes it**.
Narrow it to three: **`bunt`, `slap`, `slash`; absent = conventional.** The existing five conflate two
axes — what she intended (bunt/slap/slash) and whether she offered (showed vs pulled, slap vs
fake_slap) — and the second **derives from the outcome**: squared with `ball`/`called_strike` means
she did not offer, `foul_bunt`/`in_play` means she did. Storing it is storing a derived fact (§13),
and redundancy that can contradict its own source. Keep `slash` explicit even though
bunt-posture-plus-non-bunt-trajectory nearly implies it: that only works when she makes contact, and
the deception is the whole scouting point.

`slap` is softball-only and gates through **`RuleSet`**, never `if (softball)` — the narrow-placeholder
pattern `zone_canvas.dart` and `GameState.uncaughtThirdStrikeLive` already use.

**Wild pitch / passed ball, with a one-tap consequence.** Checking either should offer **"all runners
up one"** in a single tap, because that is the whole play the overwhelming majority of the time and it
should not require a trip to the field. It cannot *auto*-apply the way a walk's forced chain does
(§11.3: "automatic where the rules leave no doubt, a prompt where they don't") — a runner on third
often holds on a ball that only trickled away — so it is **one tap, not zero**, with action-scoped
undo as the correction rather than a confirmation dialog.

Going to the field afterwards should **seed possession loose** rather than to the catcher. Carry that
through navigation; do not infer it, because a wild pitch's physics is the *absence* of a touch
(§13.2) and there is nothing on the pitch to read.

Both consume DIA-008d's machinery — the advance construction and the passed-ball pair with touch
reuse — rather than reinventing them.

## Changed since the decisions were taken

- **`swinging_strike_blocked` is already gone** (DIA-008c). Nothing to do.
- **D3K arming already shipped** (DIA-008c). Nothing to do.
- **The sheet now has a sixth primary**: `Dropped 3rd strike`, shown only at two strikes with the
  batter entitled to run, opening a two-question flow. It is an outcome that opens a surface, the
  same category as In play — so 5A's tiering has to place it, and "five primaries" above is really
  five plus one conditional.

## Accept

- Entering a pitch never consults a location suggestion; `suggestOutcome` is deleted, not bypassed.
- The five primaries are present at fixed positions regardless of where the pitch was located.
- `batterAction` has a producer: bunt/slap/slash enterable, slap absent under a baseball `RuleSet`,
  and the value round-trips through the stream into the projection.
- A wild pitch or passed ball with runners aboard is fully enterable **without leaving the sheet**,
  and one undo reverses the whole chain.
- Fixtures and the DIA-009 script updated for the new tap counts.

## Out of scope

Colors and styling (post-M1 design ticket). Count-consequence subtitles. The pitch-entry ⇄ field
navigation question — whether the field becomes the loop's home surface for a scorer capturing
neither calls nor locations — which is a real question raised during DIA-008d and deliberately left
open here.

## Spec protocol

§11.1's "one big confirm carrying Diamond's suggestion, and the full override row beneath it" is no
longer true, and §4.1's `batterAction` enum narrows. Both need the three-place protocol (header
version, full `docs/spec-history.md` entry, status line rotation) plus the two version refs in
`CLAUDE.md`. The schema change is §21.5's one atomic commit: edit schema → regenerate Dart and TS →
update fixtures and tests.
