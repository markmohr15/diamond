# DIA-005 — Zone canvas widget
**Spec:** §3.1, §3.3, §10.1 (v0.18), §11.1 (v0.19), §18.7
**Goal:** CustomPaint strike-zone canvas: renders zone rect + out-of-zone margin; long-press-drag-release
→ ZoneCoord (absolute, catcher's-view, always — no handedness flip: `intendedLocation`/`actualLocation`
are batter-agnostic by design, see §3.1); shows tap marker, re-grabbable for quick correction; a visible
Cancel/Back button (`onCancel`), available in both modes and distinct from the skip affordance — lets the
parent step (DIA-007's pitch loop) reverse a locked-in call/actual and return to the previous step, since
nothing is committed to the event stream until the full pitch resolves (§10.3); supports underlay slot
(future §18.6); "skip location" affordance; presentational `call`/`actual` mode labeling (no zone-grid
snapping — that's DIA-006/007). Tablet + phone layouts.
**Accept:** widget tests: tap→coord math incl. out-of-zone margin, long-press/drag/release commit,
release-outside-bounds cancel, re-grab-to-correct, skip affordance, Cancel/Back button fires `onCancel` in
both modes, mode labeling; golden tests for both form factors. Design language §18.7 review pass.
**Design constraint for DIA-011:** this ticket doesn't implement the pitch-bounce hinge, but keep the
canvas's coordinate mapping and background as swappable parameters, not hardcoded to the zone rectangle —
see DIA-011 for why and the gesture semantics that depend on it.
