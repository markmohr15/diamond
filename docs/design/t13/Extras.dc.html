THE RULE

Solid is required. Dashed is optional, and never asks.

Every node in the chain carries one dashed + chip, and it opens only what makes sense at that point (§15.3). Each chip names what it opens, so the scorer can see what she could add without being asked to add it. Grounder to short, 6-3, runner from first to second.

PLAY
R1 → 2B on the hit
Batter out at 1B 6-3
WHAT EACH CHIP OPENS
+ misplay on a touch: Dropped, Booted, Bobbled, Deflected, Wild throw, Missed catch, Missed tag. A misplay turns the node amber.
+ arrival · misplay on a reception: Short hop, High, Wide, plus the throw was wild, which retypes the thrower. An arrival underlines the node and isn't a fault.
+ call · re-link on a runner's line, the batter's included: obstruction, interference, ground rule, dead-ball award; or move her advance to another touch.
+ fair/foul · fan on the ⚾: a fair/foul reversal and spectator interference. Infield fly joins them only when it's legal.
ON THE FIELD
The landing leaves a dashed ring. Dragging out of it traces the roll and records retrieved. A routine ball skips it, and nothing is lost.
