Field screen decisions
Top bar = game. Count, outs, score, inning, and one labelled UNDO PITCH 4, the same as Turn 10. The bare ↺ ✕ pair is gone.
Bottom bar = this play. Four labelled buttons in two groups. On the left, the ones that throw work away: Leave field and Start play over. On the right, under the thumb: Back one, which names the entry it removes (here SS catch), and Commit play. The buttons that discard work sit as far from Commit as the bar allows.
Leave field goes back to the pitch screen and drops the unsaved play. If anything is on the chain, it asks once (Discard this play?). Start play over keeps you on the field and clears the chain and runner moves, but keeps the trajectory.
In-play fielders are now Grass-filled chips in the chain only. On the field, the Grass fill belongs to the holder alone. See 11B.

Rail decisions
Back to game: removed. There's nothing behind the pitch loop, so a button pointing back would lead nowhere. Lineup and settings belong in the app's own menu, not on this screen.
Step 2 is folded into step 1. Tapping the code card (10B) completes CALL. The rail now has two stops, and the finished stop shows the full receipt: Drop · low middle · 702 ✓. Record-only entry (the step-3 jump) leaves stop 1 empty and starts at 2.
Light/dark moves to the AT BAT strip. It's a labelled two-segment control at the far right, after a divider. The top bar holds only game state and undo. The toggle is a once-an-inning control, so it shouldn't sit next to the most-used button.
11F
Call grids that aren't six cells
The zone stays a fixed hatched rectangle, 17 in wide by the batter's knees-to-letters height. The team's grid divides that rectangle into its configured rows × columns, and cells outside the zone are allowed for chase calls. A spot that can be called for the chosen type is filled Grass, and the others stay hatched. Spots are never resized to fill space, so a 4-cell team gets big targets and a 12-cell team gets small ones. The minimum is 64px.

3 × 3 + CHASE CELLS
Chase cells sit outside the zone border: below it for dirt balls, beside it for off the plate. A configured chase cell that's callable is filled Grass. One that isn't callable for this type is drawn dashed, so the shape of the team's grid stays visible.
