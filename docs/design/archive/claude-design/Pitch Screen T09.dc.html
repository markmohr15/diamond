Turn 09 · The card decides what you can call
Pick the pitch, and the field shows your menu
Because a card only carries so many pairs, the callable spots are a property of the pitch. Tap Drop and the zone redraws: six spots outlined in Grass with their code printed in the cell, everything else hatched as NO CODE. 9A is that menu; 9B is a spot taken, the cell flipped to Ink and the code repeated large top-left. This stays inside step 1 — it’s the same call, made in two taps, so the rail still reads three steps.

The consequence worth arguing about: the code is now legible on the grid before you ever reach step 2, so step 2 has no lookup left to do — it’s only the “I’ve shouted it” check. That may mean it shouldn’t be a screen at all, just a confirm on this one.
