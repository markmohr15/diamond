Turn 05 · The two taps after the pitch
Sheets that rank by how often they get tapped
The old sheets are eleven equal rows in a scrolling list, so a called strike costs the same attention as catcher's interference. 5A tiers the result sheet: four outcomes cover almost every pitch, so they get the thumb zone and carry the count they produce — a strike on 1–2 reads STRIKEOUT, not "2 strikes." Rare calls survive as a chip row at the bottom. 5B is the batted-ball question that fires before the play editor — six words, six equal targets, no diagrams; anyone charting a game already knows what a popup is.
